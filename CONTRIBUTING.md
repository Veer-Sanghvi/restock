# Contributing

Thanks for looking under the hood. This repo is maintained by one mechanical engineering student, and pull requests are welcome when they make the analysis more honest or more useful.

PRs that get merged fast:

- Corrections with a source: a number, unit, or claim that is wrong, with where the right value comes from.
- Reproduction reports: you ran it, something differed from the README, you say what and on which platform.
- Stated-limits additions: an assumption the README does not state yet.
- Figure and doc clarity fixes.

PRs that will not merge: drive-by reformatting, dependency bumps without a reason, and anything that trades honesty for polish. Keep the no-em-dash house style in prose.

Open an issue first for anything bigger than a small fix. Be direct; short reviews are kind reviews.
